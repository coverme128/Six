# AI Image Style Guide

AI-generated or rights-cleared imagery may be used for:
- cultural/historical atmosphere,
- article hero,
- learning context,
- abstract traditional-culture scenes.

AI imagery must **never** encode deterministic Liuyao facts such as:
- actual hexagram lines,
- moving-line location,
- Shi/Ying,
- Yongshen,
- strength state,
- Evidence,
- Pattern,
- Timing.

Professional fact visuals must be deterministic React/SVG from ViewModels.
